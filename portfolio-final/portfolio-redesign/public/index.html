About the project

A GAME NIGHT is an original first-person horror game centered around a seemingly ordinary family game night that takes a horrifying turn.

After discovering their family dead around the dining table, the protagonist encounters a mysterious, sinister Jester who treats the entire ordeal as a game. What begins as a desperate attempt to escape gradually becomes a disturbing experience involving exploration, hiding, psychological tension, and the feeling that the player may never truly escape.

The game aims to create an unsettling atmosphere through cinematic storytelling, environmental design, carefully crafted sound, and an antagonist whose playful behaviour makes him even more unpredictable.

Development goals

Cinematic storytelling. A scripted opening sequence introduces the protagonist, establishes the atmosphere, and reveals the Jester before transitioning into player-controlled gameplay.

Atmospheric level design. A compact apartment and residential building will serve as the primary environment, using lighting, environmental storytelling, and sound to build tension.

Enemy design and behaviour. The Jester is designed as a distinctive antagonist who toys with the player rather than relying exclusively on constant pursuit. His encounters will combine stalking, scripted scares, and carefully timed chase sequences.

Interactive gameplay. Planned mechanics include first-person exploration, object interaction, key-based progression, hiding, security-camera investigation, and escape sequences.

Original audio direction. The soundscape will feature a slow, unsettling piano theme alongside the Jester’s signature laughter, metallic bells, and delicate porcelain sounds.

Technical direction

The planned development pipeline combines Unity for gameplay and scene management, C# for game systems, Blender for 3D modelling and cinematic production, and AI-assisted 3D tools for generating and refining initial assets.

The project is being approached as a solo game-development undertaking, with an emphasis on building a manageable, polished experience rather than an unnecessarily large game.

Current progress

The project is in its early development stage. Current work centres on establishing the game’s concept, narrative, antagonist design, cinematic direction, audio identity, and technical workflow.

The next milestones are to develop a playable prototype, implement first-person movement and interaction, construct the initial environment, and progressively build the opening cinematic and core gameplay systems.

Vision

The goal is to create a compact but memorable horror experience in which atmosphere, sound, and the antagonist’s unsettling personality carry as much weight as the gameplay itself.

The game is still in development, and its mechanics, environments, and cinematics will evolve throughout production.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information this website collects and how it is used. The site is a personal portfolio run by Divyajyot Sinha, who is responsible for the information described here. Contact: divyajyotsinha10@gmail.com.

What I collect

  • Contact form. If you use the form, I receive the name, email address and message you type in. I use them only to read and reply to your message.
  • Direct email or phone. If you email or call me, I receive whatever you choose to share.
  • Fonts. The site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser requests them from Google's servers, which may see your IP address and browser details.
  • Server logs. The hosting provider may record technical data such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and reliability.

What I do not do

  • This site does not use advertising trackers or analytics.
  • It does not set cookies of its own.
  • I do not sell your information or share it for marketing.

How messages are handled

Contact form messages are sent to my email inbox through an email service provider. The site does not keep them in a database. I keep a message only as long as I need it to respond or follow up, and you can ask me to delete it at any time.

Third parties

The site links to outside services, such as GitHub and LinkedIn. Those services have their own privacy practices and I am not responsible for them. The email and hosting providers I use process data on my behalf to deliver the service.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information I hold about you by emailing me. I will reply within a reasonable time.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated.
